Hi! I'm Gabriel, a CS undergrad at the University of São Paulo, where I have been doing research since 2023 with [Prof. Diego Furtado Silva](https://diegofurts.github.io){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. Recently, I was a visiting researcher at the Universidad de Granada with [Prof. Isaac Triguero](https://people.cs.nott.ac.uk/pszit/index.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. My research interests are, broadly speaking, time series learning, foundation models, and efficient deep learning, with a focus on time series classification and extrinsic regression.
